# Video Wallpaper

A simple Android app that lets you set any video from your phone as your live home screen and lock screen wallpaper.

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

---

## What It Does

- **Turn Any Video into Wallpaper**: Choose any video saved on your phone and make it your live wallpaper.
- **Preview Before Applying**: Watch a quick preview of your video inside the app before setting it.
- **Battery-Friendly**: Automatically pauses the video when your screen is turned off or when you are using other apps, so it won't drain your battery.
- **Muted by Default**: Plays cleanly in the background without sound so it won't disturb your music, calls, or notifications.
- **Looping**: Automatically repeats your video seamlessly.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Open the App**: Launch **Video Wallpaper** on your phone.
2. **Import a Video**: Tap **Import Video** to select a video file from your phone's gallery or downloads.
3. **Select & Preview**: Tap on your imported video in the list to preview it at the top of the screen.
4. **Apply Wallpaper**: Tap the **Apply as Live Wallpaper** button and confirm on the Android wallpaper screen.

---

## Settings

- **Loop Video**: Keep this turned on to have your video continuously repeat on your home screen.
- **In-App GitHub Updates**: Check for new releases directly from GitHub, download updates right inside the app, and install them with one tap.

---

## Permissions

- **Photos & Videos / Storage**: Needed only to allow you to pick and import videos stored on your device. Your videos stay 100% on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
